# CropBot fact extraction, prompt v1

You read one section of an Indian crop production guide and write down every piece of agronomic advice in it as structured facts. A program checks your answer: it parses it into a fixed model and looks for every quote in the section text. Nothing you write is corrected; anything you get wrong goes to a human reviewer. When the text does not say something, leave the field empty. Never fill a gap from your own knowledge.

## Your answer

Answer with a JSON array of facts and nothing else: no prose, no code fences. Each fact is a JSON object with the fields in "Field reference". Leave out fields you do not fill. If the section holds no advice, answer `[]`.

## The input

The section is given at the end, after "SECTION". Its lines are:

- `[p.N] text`: a paragraph or bullet printed on PDF page N.
- `[p.N] ## Heading`: an unnumbered sub-heading printed in the section.
- `[p.N] TABLE #/tables/A + #/tables/B`: a table, joined across pages when it continues.
- `[#/tables/A header] cell | cell`: the table's header.
- `[#/tables/A row R] cell | cell`: row R of table part A; cells are separated by " | ".

The markers in square brackets, the "##" and the "TABLE" lines are not part of the text. Never copy them into a quote.

The crop and the book's region come from the program; do not add them. Approved default-scope rules for this section are listed under "DEFAULT-SCOPE RULES".

## Rules

1. **One fact per piece of advice.** A fact is one subtype, one set of applicability (varieties, seasons, systems, soils, purposes, stages, duration group) and one source passage. Split advice that differs in applicability: a seed rate given per duration group is one fact per group. Keep the ordered steps of one procedure in one fact. Advice repeated in two places is two facts.
2. **Quote exactly.** `quote` is one contiguous passage copied character for character from the text: same words, spelling, numbers, spacing and typos. It may run across consecutive lines, but never skip text in between and never paraphrase. It must contain every number the fact uses. For a fact from a table, quote one cell's text, or the cells of one row as shown, joined by " | "; set `table_ref` to the table part of that row and `table_row` to its row number R (and `table_col` when one cell, counted from 0, holds the fact).
3. **Names as printed.** Write chemicals, fertilizers, manures, pests, diseases, weeds, varieties, seasons, systems, soils, stages and regions exactly as the text writes them ("DAP", "Pseudomonas fluorescens", "BPH", "clayey soils"). Do not translate them, expand abbreviations or turn them into IDs.
4. **Numbers.** Parse numbers into `value`, or into `min` and `max` for a range ("8 to 10", "20-25", "3 rd or 4 th day"). Number words become numbers ("one tonne" = 1, "Five packets" = 5). "less than", "below", "within" give a max; "more than", "above", "after" give a min; set the matching `_inclusive` flag to false when the bound itself is excluded ("less than 8 %"). Keep the printed text: `raw` on a quantity holds the whole quantity phrase; on a fact, `raw` maps each numeric subtype field to its printed text, e.g. `{"target_moisture_pct_max": "12%"}`.
5. **Never convert units.** `unit` is the unit as printed ("kg", "g", "ml", "tonne", "l", "%"). Subtype fields whose name carries a unit (`_cm`, `_pct`, `_months`, `_per_m2`) are filled only when the text uses that unit; otherwise leave them empty and put the phrase in `unmapped`.
6. **Basis.** `basis` is what the amount is per, as printed: "/ha", "per 20 cents", "/kg of seed", "/l of water", "/cent". For "2 g/l of water for 1 kg of seeds" the unit is "g" and the basis is "/l of water for 1 kg of seeds". When no basis is printed with the amount:
   - if a DEFAULT-SCOPE RULE covers it, set `basis` to the rule's basis and `basis_rule` to the rule text exactly as listed;
   - else, if the section itself states what the amounts are for (for example the nursery area for one hectare), set `basis` to that, as the section words it, and `basis_inferred` to true;
   - else leave `basis` empty.
7. **Amounts stated earlier.** If a step applies something whose amount was given in an earlier bullet ("application of DAP is to be done 10 days prior…"), give the quantity with `target`, no amount, and `quantity_ref` "previous". Never copy the earlier amount.
8. **Alternatives.** Options joined by "or", "(or)", "/" or "any one of" are steps with the same `order` and `alt_group`, numbered by `alt_option` 1, 2, …; several things applied together in one option are several quantities of one step. A fallback ("if not readily available, apply …") gets `alt_priority` 2 with the fallback words in `condition_raw`; the preferred option gets `alt_priority` 1. Plain equal options have no `alt_priority`.
9. **Conditions.** Use a condition only when a listed condition variable expresses it (for example "when spider is present at 1/hill" is `spider_count_per_hill` `>=` 1). Counts stay numbers. Any other "if", "when" or "where" goes in `condition_raw` of the step it governs, or in `unmapped` when it governs the whole fact.
10. **Scope in the text.** Put a region in `regions` only when the text limits this advice to it. "other than X" goes in `excluded_regions` with the whole phrase in `raw`. Set `duration_group` when the advice is for short, medium or long duration crops or varieties.
11. **Steps and timing.** `operation` comes from the list; leave it empty when none fits (do not force one). `anchor` is what the timing counts from; offsets are negative before it ("10 days prior to pulling out" = anchor "pulling_out", `offset_min` -10, `offset_max` -10). "DAS" means days after sowing. Use `offset_unit` "hours" for hours ("18 to 24 hrs after sowing"). A month-based window uses anchor "calendar" with `month_start`/`month_end`. Whenever timing is set, copy its words into `timing_raw`. Durations of an action ("soak for 10 hrs", "shade dry for 30 minutes") have no field: put them in `unmapped`.
12. **unmapped.** List every phrase of advice in the quote that no field holds: limits, qualifiers, durations, conditions with no variable, dimensions. Do not list explanations or reasons ("as draining hastens maturity").
13. **What is not a fact.** Explanations, definitions and statements that advise nothing are not facts, unless a subtype holds them (an incompatibility statement is a Caution). Advice that fits no subtype is not output.
14. **summary_en** is one plain English line saying what the fact advises.

## Subtypes

Allowed in this run: {{subtypes}}

- **VarietyRecommendation**: varieties recommended for a place, season or system (`recommends`).
- **SowingWindow**: when to sow, by month and day.
- **SeedRate**: how much seed; the rate is a step quantity with `target` "seed" (or the seed as printed).
- **Spacing**: row and plant spacing, hills per m², seedlings per hill.
- **SeedTreatment**: treating seed before sowing; steps apply the agents.
- **NutrientDose**: manures and fertilizers; each step has its own timing and quantities. `dose_basis` is "blanket" unless the dose depends on a soil test or a leaf colour chart.
- **DoseFormula**: a fertilizer prescription equation (targeted yield).
- **IrrigationRule**: water depth, irrigation and draining (including draining before harvest).
- **PestThreshold**: when a pest or disease is bad enough to act (economic threshold); one clause per alternative threshold.
- **ControlMeasure**: controlling a pest, disease, weed or disorder; `control_class` and steps. Weed control is a ControlMeasure whose target is a weed.
- **WeatherRule**: what to do or avoid under given weather.
- **HarvestRule**: maturity signs, moisture at harvest or threshing, and harvest timing.
- **DryingSpec**: drying to a target moisture, for a storage term or purpose.
- **StorageRule**: storage period, moisture limit, structure, protectants and storage pests.
- **QualitySpec**: regulatory quality limits.
- **Caution**: prohibitions, safety warnings, incompatibilities, banned products, waiting periods.

## Field reference

Every fact:

- `subtype`: one of the allowed subtypes.
- `summary_en`: rule 14.
- `quote`: rule 2.
- `table_ref`, `table_row`, `table_col`: rule 2, only for facts from a table.
- `duration_group`: rule 10.
- `varieties`, `seasons`, `systems`, `soils`, `purposes`, `stages`: lists of names as printed, only when the advice is limited to them. Purposes are grain, seed, fodder or green manure.
- `regions`, `excluded_regions`: rule 10. An excluded region has `name` (as printed) and `raw` (the whole phrase).
- `unmapped`: rule 12.
- `raw`: rule 4.
- `steps`: ordered steps (below).
- `conditions`: conditions on the whole fact (below).

A step (`steps`):

- `order`: 1, 2, … in the order they are done.
- `alt_group`, `alt_option`, `alt_priority`, `condition_raw`: rule 8.
- `operation`, `anchor`, `offset_min`, `offset_max`, `offset_min_inclusive`, `offset_max_inclusive`, `offset_unit`, `month_start`, `month_end`, `timing_raw`: rule 11.
- `stage`: a growth stage as printed ("panicle initiation"), with `timing_raw`.
- `method`: how it is done, in a few words ("broadcast", "foliar spray", "shade drying").
- `instruction_en`: the step in one plain English sentence.
- `applies`: quantities (below).
- `conditions`: conditions on this step only.

A quantity (`applies`):

- `target`: what is applied, as printed.
- `value`, or `min` and `max`, with `min_inclusive`, `max_inclusive`: rule 4.
- `unit`: rule 5.
- `basis`, `basis_inferred`, `basis_rule`: rule 6.
- `formulation`: the formulation as printed ("50 WP", "talc based").
- `water_raw`: the water it is mixed in or sprayed with, as printed ("500 l/ha", "1lit of water").
- `raw`: the whole quantity phrase as printed.
- `quantity_ref`: rule 7.

A condition (`conditions`):

- `variable`: one of the condition variables.
- `op`: one of the operators; "between" needs `value_max`; "in" needs a list `value`.
- `value`, `value_max`: numbers for number variables.
- `unit`: as printed, if the text gives one.
- `any_of_group`: conditions with the same number are alternatives (OR); different numbers must all hold (AND). Start at 1.
- `raw`: the condition's words as printed.

Subtype fields:

- VarietyRecommendation: `recommends` (variety names as printed), `note`.
- SowingWindow: `start_month`, `start_day`, `end_month`, `end_day` (numbers; months 1 to 12).
- SeedRate: `method` (e.g. "transplanted", "broadcast").
- Spacing: `row_cm`, `plant_cm`, `hills_per_m2`, `seedlings_per_hill`.
- NutrientDose: `dose_basis`.
- DoseFormula: `target_nutrient`, `coefficients_raw` (the equation as printed), `target_yield_unit`, `soil_series`, `valid_range`.
- IrrigationRule: `depth_cm`, `criterion` (the sign that triggers irrigation, e.g. "hairline cracks").
- PestThreshold: `pests` (names as printed) and `clauses`. A clause has `observation` (what is counted, e.g. "hoppers", "dead hearts"), `value`, `unit` (as printed, "%" or empty for a count), `sampling_unit` (as printed, "/tiller", "per hill"), `stage`, `raw` (the threshold as printed) and its own `conditions`.
- ControlMeasure: `control_class`, `targets` (names as printed).
- WeatherRule: `operation`, `advice`.
- HarvestRule: `maturity_signs` (each sign as a short phrase), `harvest_moisture_pct_min`, `harvest_moisture_pct_max`, `threshing_moisture_pct_min`, `threshing_moisture_pct_max`, and their `harvest_moisture_pct_min_inclusive`, `harvest_moisture_pct_max_inclusive`, `threshing_moisture_pct_min_inclusive`, `threshing_moisture_pct_max_inclusive` flags.
- DryingSpec: `target_moisture_pct_min`, `target_moisture_pct_max`, `storage_months_min`, `storage_months_max`, their `target_moisture_pct_min_inclusive`, `target_moisture_pct_max_inclusive`, `storage_months_min_inclusive`, `storage_months_max_inclusive` flags, `storage_term_raw` ("short term", "long term" as printed) and `method`. "Dry to 12%" is `target_moisture_pct_max` 12; "dry to 12-13 %" is min 12 and max 13. A term given only in words ("long term") keeps the months empty.
- StorageRule: `storage_months_min`, `storage_months_max`, `max_moisture_pct`, their `storage_months_min_inclusive`, `storage_months_max_inclusive`, `max_moisture_pct_inclusive` flags, `structure` (as printed) and `targets` (storage pests as printed). Protectants are step quantities.
- QualitySpec: `authority`, `valid_for_season`, `parameter`, `limit_value`, `unit`.
- Caution: `kind`, `text_en` (the caution in one plain sentence), `concerns` (active ingredients as printed).

## Example

This example is invented to show the format; it is not from this book.

Text:

```
[p.40] Apply 60 kg of urea/ha at transplanting and 30 kg at panicle initiation.
[p.40] If urea is not available, apply 140 kg of ammonium sulphate/ha at transplanting.
```

Answer:

```
[{"subtype": "NutrientDose", "dose_basis": "blanket",
  "summary_en": "Apply 60 kg urea/ha at transplanting (or 140 kg ammonium sulphate/ha if urea is not available) and 30 kg urea at panicle initiation.",
  "quote": "Apply 60 kg of urea/ha at transplanting and 30 kg at panicle initiation. If urea is not available, apply 140 kg of ammonium sulphate/ha at transplanting.",
  "steps": [
    {"order": 1, "alt_group": 1, "alt_option": 1, "alt_priority": 1, "operation": "fertilizer_application",
     "anchor": "transplanting", "offset_min": 0, "offset_max": 0, "timing_raw": "at transplanting",
     "instruction_en": "Apply 60 kg urea per ha at transplanting.",
     "applies": [{"target": "urea", "value": 60, "unit": "kg", "basis": "/ha", "raw": "60 kg of urea/ha"}]},
    {"order": 1, "alt_group": 1, "alt_option": 2, "alt_priority": 2, "condition_raw": "If urea is not available",
     "operation": "fertilizer_application", "anchor": "transplanting", "offset_min": 0, "offset_max": 0,
     "timing_raw": "at transplanting", "instruction_en": "If urea is not available, apply 140 kg ammonium sulphate per ha at transplanting.",
     "applies": [{"target": "ammonium sulphate", "value": 140, "unit": "kg", "basis": "/ha", "raw": "140 kg of ammonium sulphate/ha"}]},
    {"order": 2, "operation": "fertilizer_application", "stage": "panicle initiation", "timing_raw": "at panicle initiation",
     "instruction_en": "Apply 30 kg urea at panicle initiation.",
     "applies": [{"target": "urea", "value": 30, "unit": "kg", "basis": "/ha", "basis_inferred": true, "raw": "30 kg"}]}]}]
```

The quote runs across both lines because the fallback belongs to the same dose. "30 kg" has no basis printed with it, so "/ha" from the same sentence is marked `basis_inferred`.
